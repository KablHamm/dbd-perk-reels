Survivor perks

Survivors I own

Untick a character to leave their perks out of spins and suggested builds. Tick them again when you get them. General perks are always included. Saved on this device.

Survivor challenges

Click a challenge to tick it off, and again to untick it. Resetting clears the ticks so you can do them again; your achievements stay unlocked.

Survivor achievements

An achievement unlocks the first time you complete its challenge, and stays unlocked even if you untick or reset your challenges. Saved on this device.
